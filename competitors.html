Fold, INDmoney and CRED Money

Fold is the only one of the three where tracking money is the product. INDmoney and CRED give tracking away free to sell investing and credit, so Fold has to win on accuracy and depth, not price.

Market
Money tracking, India
Apps
Fold · INDmoney · CRED Money
Sources
Public pages, reviews, the Fold app
Date
31 Oct 2026

  • Question

    Why would anyone pay for Fold when rivals track money for free?

  • Answer

    Only if Fold is clearly more accurate and complete. Trust is the product.

  • So what

    Stale data hurts Fold more than its rivals, which backs the bank-sync alert.

The comparison

All three pull bank data through India's Account Aggregator network, so the difference is what each app is for.

FoldINDmoneyCRED Money
Target userPeople who want every account, card and investment tracked automatically, often ex-spreadsheet usersInvestors in Indian and US stocks and mutual fundsAffluent, creditworthy users; joining CRED needed a credit score of 750 or more
Core featureAutomatic tracking: auto-tagged spending, cash flow and net worthInvesting, with a net worth view that includes bank accountsOne view of all bank balances, plus reminders for recurring payments
PricingFree tier, plus Fold Plus at ₹999 a year (seen in the app, 31 Oct 2026)Tracking has no listed fee; earns from brokerageFree; CRED said it won't charge for it
Where it falls shortStale bank data isn't dated on balances and totals; paywall angers long-time users; card tracking needs email accessTracking is a side feature, better at totals than day-to-day spendingOnly for CRED's audience; tracking sits inside a large payments app

What it means for Fold

Fold charges for something its two rivals give away. That only works if Fold's tracking is clearly more accurate and complete than a free side feature.

  • Trust is the product. A stale bank feed hurts Fold more than it hurts INDmoney or CRED, because tracking is all Fold sells. That backs the bank-sync alert.
  • The paywall needs to feel fair. Pricing was the angriest theme in Fold's reviews. With free alternatives a tap away, moving features from free to paid pushes users out.
  • Depth is the moat. Fold tracks cards, EPF, NPS, FDs and insurance in one place. Neither rival makes that its main job, so Fold should keep widening coverage.
