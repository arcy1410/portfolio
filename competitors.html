Fold, INDmoney and CRED Money

Fold is the only one of the three where tracking money is the product. INDmoney and CRED give tracking away free to sell investing and credit, so Fold has to win on accuracy and depth, not price.

Evidence

Same data pipes, different businesses

All three pull bank data through India's Account Aggregator network, so the difference is what each app is for.

Fold

People who want every account, card and investment tracked automatically, often ex-spreadsheet users

INDmoney

Investors in Indian and US stocks and mutual funds

CRED Money

Affluent, creditworthy users; joining CRED needed a credit score of 750 or more

Fold

Automatic tracking: auto-tagged spending, cash flow and net worth

INDmoney

Investing, with a net worth view that includes bank accounts

CRED Money

One view of all bank balances, plus reminders for recurring payments

Fold

Free tier, plus Fold Plus at ₹999 a year (seen in the app, 31 Oct 2026)

INDmoney

Tracking has no listed fee; earns from brokerage

CRED Money

Free; CRED said it won't charge for it

Fold

Stale bank data isn't dated on balances and totals; the paywall angers long-time users; card tracking needs email access

INDmoney

Tracking is a side feature, better at totals than day-to-day spending

CRED Money

Only for CRED's audience; tracking sits inside a large payments app

Decision

What it means for Fold

Fold charges for something its two rivals give away. That only works if Fold's tracking is clearly more accurate and complete than a free side feature.

  • Trust is the productA stale bank feed hurts Fold more than it hurts INDmoney or CRED, because tracking is all Fold sells. That backs the bank-sync alert.
  • The paywall needs to feel fairPricing was the angriest theme in Fold's reviews. With free alternatives a tap away, moving features from free to paid pushes users out.
  • Depth is the moatFold tracks cards, EPF, NPS, FDs and insurance in one place. Neither rival makes that its main job, so Fold should keep widening coverage.
  • Price is not the fightFold can't undercut free. It has to be the app people trust when the numbers matter.
