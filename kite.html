Opening a Zerodha Kite account: 19 screens in 7 minutes

What works

What Kite gets right

For a regulated brokerage account, 7 minutes is fast, and most of the flow is well judged.

  • KYC does the typingOnce I entered my PAN, my address came from existing KYC records, so I picked it instead of typing it.
  • Plain words at anxious moments“We will not send you any physical documents unless you ask us to.”
  • Optional steps can waitNominee and F&O income proof both have a clear “later” option.
  • I could start right awayA user ID arrived the moment I finished, and I could explore Kite while the application was under review.

Fixes

If I were the PM

Three fixes, from mostly copy to the most work.

  1. An honest progress barShow time left, and group password and 2FA under “Finish setup” rather than after “Congratulations”. Mostly copy and layout.
  2. One place to sign upKeep sign-up inside the app, and give the e-sign hand-off a sentence of explanation. The most work of the three, and it removes the most drop-off points.
  3. A beginner-safe defaultStart with stocks and mutual funds, and offer F&O later. A small screen change with a real protective effect.

Measure

How I'd measure them

Track completion at every screen, so drop-off at each hand-off shows up.

Plus the median time from opening the app to the first watchlist. As a guardrail, I'd watch the share of new users who switch on F&O in their first week, since fewer is the intended effect of fix 3, not a loss.

Method and limits: one person on one run, by someone who already uses fintech apps, so a nervous first-time investor likely feels more friction. Timings come from the screenshots' clock. Screenshots aren't published because they contain personal details.
